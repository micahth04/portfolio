About

Micah Reyes is a reporter in St. Louis. He writes about how money moves through city government and who it reaches. His work has led to two state audits and a change in municipal housing code.

Before joining the Post-Dispatch he spent four years at the Riverfront Times, where he covered courts. He grew up in Overland and studied at Mizzou.

He is open to freelance assignments and can be reached by email or on Signal. Tips are welcome and treated as confidential. Résumé available on request.
